ChordLayout::appendGraceNotes selects a following segment at the owning chord's
end tick in the same measure, skipping time-tick and staff-empty segments.
There is no chord/rest-only target restriction. A normal ending barline is a
valid target in the focused cases. Do not attach these notes to the next
measure's first chord or treat the barline as a rest.

TLayout::layoutGraceNotesGroup packs the whole group right-to-left, then uses
the appended segment's staff shape for group placement. Its grace-to-main
safety bound still applies. HorizontalSpacing::computePadding scales ordinary
table padding by the mean item magnification. Its special grace-to-main
minimum applies to note/rest targets, not barlines: isSpecialNotePaddingType
includes note/rest/stem, not barline. Thus grace-note-to-barline clearance uses
the noteBarDistance table entry and ordinary scaling, alongside the group
safety bound and other typed components. Copying the rest rule is insufficient.
Evolution already has ordinary typed barline geometry/padding; the missing
piece here is validated after-grace target/projection/publication support.

For a target chord with its own before-graces, appendGraceNotes appends those
before-graces after the incoming after-graces in one pre-appended group. The
group is packed as a single accumulated shape against the target staff shape.
It retains each member's musical parent. repositionGraceNotesAfter translates
members from appended-segment to their respective owning-segment coordinates
after horizontal spacing. Independently packing the two groups can omit the
after-to-before constraint or reserve clearance twice. Mixed directions also
invoke the source group's head-width-minus-stem-width correction.
