## Verified source audit: different stretch inputs

The pinned Studio 4.7.5 loader's read302 reader delegates staff reading to
read400. MeasureRW explicitly skips the stretch tag for file versions below
400. The DOM Measure constructor initializes m_userStretch to 1.0. Our fixture
is version 3.02 and stores stretch 2, which Evolution reads and honors.
Therefore the screenshots compare stretch 2 in Evolution with stretch 1 in
Studio. They cannot establish a discrepancy between equal-input squeeze runs.
