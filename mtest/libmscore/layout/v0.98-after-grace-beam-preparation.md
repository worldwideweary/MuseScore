## Source finding and targeted correction

Score::layoutPage lays out grace beams after horizontal system placement.
Beam::layout adjusts stem lengths and rebuilds native segment shapes. Existing
early preparation covers before-graces only, leaving the new after-grace full
planner exposed to provisional stem geometry. The report establishes a plan
mismatch, but contains no numeric geometry diagnostics to prove this is its
only cause. Native confirmation is required.

Extend early preparation only to groups admitted by buildGraceAfterGeometry4.
For a beamed group, temporarily place its chords at planned relative distances,
anchored to the original first grace x, then lay out the beam. Restore every
native chord x and refresh its source segment shape before rebuilding the
read-only projection. This changes preparation geometry, not musical ownership
or final grace placement. Final publication remains with the successful system
plan; unsupported groups retain the existing fallback path.

Require source and target component identities and rectangles to stabilize
within 1e-9, with a maximum of eight attempts. Packing and beam geometry can
depend on each other; merely laying out at collapsed native positions would
not establish the required geometry. Failure to rebuild or stabilize disables
the full fitting/placement plan for that pass. Unbeamed supported groups need
no beam preparation. Spacing-off and no-horizontal-stretch skip preparation.

Preserve the failing tests and add short numeric diagnostics to their natural
width and compressed segment-x assertions. These distinguish a remaining
geometry mismatch from a small numerical discrepancy in another native report.
