The user's v0.112 slurred examples expose a coverage gap. Acceptance of the
ordinary grace stage applied to the tested slur-free inputs, not all common
slurred ornaments. Blue image(20261024-093733).png versus white
image(20261024-093804).png and their detail crops show spacing differences and
separate inferior legacy curve placement. Do not describe the entire slurred

## Confirmed spacing cause and source audit

commonItemContext4 only allowed one narrowly scoped first-before-grace-to-main
slur. Cross-group after-to-target slurs failed that guard. The combined query
also called the independent before planner as a validation step, exposing it
to that narrower scope. Adding the depicted slurs could reject the combined
projection and whole-system plan, returning to the older spacing path.

Pinned Studio 4.7.5 horizontalspacing.cpp, blob
573e19dcf63bb042499f1495f16f5c19d1003852, has no direct slur padding case. Its
explicit line-attachment minimum-length branch covers ties, glissandi, bends
and note lines. This supports fixing eligibility before inventing a slur gap.
The new tests check that these automatic slurs preserve the existing new
horizontal layout; this is not a universal claim about all slurred notation.
