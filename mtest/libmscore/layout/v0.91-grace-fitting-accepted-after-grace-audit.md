## Why after-graces require a separate adapter

Studio's chord still owns its after-graces, but appendGraceNotes pre-appends
their group to a following segment found at the chord's end tick within the
measure. It skips time-tick segments and segments with no items on that staff.
Do not replace that lookup with an unconditional next chord-rest or next
measure lookup. A missing target needs explicit fallback.

Before-graces attach to their own segment. If that segment already carries
the previous chord's after-graces in the same track, Studio merges the groups.
Consequently, a following chord with its own before-graces is a distinct case,
not two independent applications of the current before-grace adapter.

MeasureLayout::updateGraceNotes clears earlier pre-appended groups and
refreshes affected shapes, appends current groups, then packs each group with
TLayout::layoutGraceNotesGroup. That packing works right-to-left against an
accumulated group shape, then against the target segment's staff shape.
GraceNotesGroup::addToShape publishes components into the appended segment,
including the additional visual staff for cross-staff cases.

After horizontal spacing, repositionGraceNotesAfter translates each member
by the difference between target-segment and owning-segment positions.
It does not transfer musical ownership. Applying a parent-relative x before
segment spacing, or applying that difference twice, would give incorrect
positions on repeated layout.

Evolution currently reserves aggregate right space for after-graces in
Chord::layoutPitched and positions them in Chord::layout2 after spacing, using
the following segment's staff-shape left extent and legacy distances.
buildGraceBeforePlan4 explicitly rejects any after-graces. The system planner
therefore retains legacy fallback for these contexts. That guard is intentional
and must remain until target-segment geometry and final coordinate conversion
are implemented together.
