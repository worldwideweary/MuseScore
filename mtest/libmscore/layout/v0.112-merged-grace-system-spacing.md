## Admission, geometry and publication

Before spacing, try ordinary after geometry first, then eligible joint geometry.
For an accepted combined group, prepare both independent beams and mark its
target so the ordinary before-grace pass does not overwrite that preparation.
Failed beam convergence disables optional full-system placement; native final
grace-beam layout recalculates derived geometry at the resulting positions.

The read-only system planner stages joint projections and validates all groups
before publishing its contexts. Register the incoming owner for after-graces
and the target for before-graces; skip independent before-position validation
on a target already covered by a complete joint projection. Keep original
after-only and merged plan/result types separate.

Reuse the proven substitution validator with an explicit target-before mode.
Source ownership permits the incoming owner and its after-graces; target
ownership permits the target and its before-graces. Unknown/mixed/foreign
components and invalid aggregate/shared provenance reject. A private temporary
view reuses validation without putting mixed-owner members in public after-only
plans. Keep after-only substitution behavior unchanged.

SpacingPlan4 retains mergedGraces through natural layout, fitting, squeezing
and justification. After final segment placement, translate each member from
target-segment coordinates to its own parent's coordinates. Refresh both native
segment shapes after all assignments. No parent or track is transferred.

Admission remains bounded by the v0.110/v0.111 guards. Opposite-direction M2,
cross-staff/voice/cue/manual/decoration and other unsupported combined contexts
remain on fallback. The existing default-off spacing style controls this work;
spacing-off rendering and the independent beam option retain their roles.
