## Layout and spacing responsibilities

1. ChordLayout::appendGraceNotes attaches before-grace groups to the current
   segment. After-graces attach to an appropriate following segment. Existing
   group items in the same voice may be combined.
2. MeasureLayout clears old appended groups and recreates affected staff
   shapes, appends current groups, lays them out, and adds their shapes back.
   Repeated layout must therefore avoid accumulating old grace geometry.
3. layoutGraceNotesGroup works from the last grace chord backward. For each,
   compute a collision distance against the accumulated group shape to its
   right, translate it and add it to that shape. This is a whole-group shape
   check, not a fixed adjacent-center gap or the sum of chord widths.
4. Its ordinary distance computation uses the existing typed padding/kerning
   machinery. Grace-to-grace note padding has a 0.3sp default floor; grace-to-
   main and main-to-grace have a separate 0.45sp floor. Notehead, accidental,
   stem, ledger-line and other supported footprints still affect the result.
5. The group is then positioned against the appended segment's staff shape,
   so the main note/chord and other items on that staff can constrain it.
   Cross-staff parent/grace contexts check an additional staff shape. Those
   paths require separate adaptation rather than being implied by plain graces.
6. A final safety bound ensures the group position is no farther right than
   the negative sum of graceToMainNoteDist and half the last notehead width. The
   expression is -(distance + headWidth/2), not just a uniform extra gap.
7. Cross-beam stem-side changes add a signed notehead/stem correction during
   group construction. TAB has linked-staff alignment and stem updates. These
   are outside the proposed first ordinary before-grace scope.
8. GraceNotesGroup::setPos moves its member chords; addToShape adds their
   positioned shapes to the appended segment. layoutGraceNotesGroup2 later
   rebuilds the group's composite shape from placed members. After-grace
   repositioning has an additional post-horizontal-spacing step.

Evolution's Chord::layoutPitched currently packs before-graces backward using
_spaceLw/_spaceRw, minNoteDistance and graceNoteMag. This establishes a real
algorithm/model difference consistent with the user's smaller internal and
larger final gap observation. It does not prove that changing the two distance
values alone reproduces the screenshot; fonts, effective styles, accidentals,
stem/slash geometry and slur settings still need matched controls.

## First implementation boundary

Start with ordinary pitched before-grace groups, one voice, single-note normal
heads, no staff moves, manual offsets, tremolos, ties or attached slurs. Prepare
item-aware group/parent shapes and derive every position read-only before
publishing any change. Allow grace context only through a validated adapter;
keep existing planner/collision rejection for other grace input until its full
system-width contribution is integrated. Preserve legacy-off handling.

Acciaccatura slash geometry needs an explicit policy: Studio permits stem-slash
collision within the same grace beam, while other collision relationships must
retain clearance. The current kernel's grace rejection cannot simply be
removed. Likewise, choose the group-to-main safety bound with correct group,
chord and segment coordinate conversions; avoid adding it twice to main-note
left space. Rebuild geometry after movement and verify repeat stability.

Create matched ordinary controls covering plain contour, sharp/flat/natural
accidentals, both stem directions and isolated terminal-gap behavior. Include
slurs in a separate follow-up after note spacing agrees. The user's images
alone are not an exact score reproduction; obtain their original score only
if controlled cases do not explain the observed behavior.
